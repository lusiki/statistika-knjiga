# Author pre-dispositions for the open Phase 2 and Phase 3 decision gates

## What this document is, and what it is not

The author answered the substantive question behind ten still-open decision
gates in one session on 2026-08-04, ahead of those gates opening. This record
exists so each gate opens with the author's intent already stated instead of
stalling on a fresh ask.

**This is not a ratification and confers no authority.** Every listed gate
still requires its own dated disposition against its own declared source
state, exactly as its contract specifies. A spine gate approves a *specific
drafted spine*; those drafts do not exist yet, so nothing here can stand in
for one. The author may revise any intent below once the actual draft is in
front of them, and doing so is a normal outcome rather than a contradiction.

The correct use is: when a gate opens, draft to the intent recorded here, put
the draft in front of the author, and expect a fast confirmation rather than a
fresh deliberation.

`G-A2d` is deliberately absent. It was closed independently on the same day by
`deb9b30`, and the author confirmed that the committed record stands. Answers
collected in this session for solutions policy, Appendix B, and the privacy
lanes were duplicates of an already-settled gate and are discarded. They must
not be revived from this document.

The two data-rights asks are also absent. `OA-G-A3-DZS-RIGHTS` and
`OA-G-A3-DIP-RIGHTS` are owned by the respective source organisations, not by
the author, and no author intent can substitute for their reply.

## Spine intents

### `G-A2b-PREFACE` — preface

The preface is a reader contract. It states what the reader will be able to
do, what is out of scope, and what is expected of them, then hands off to
Chapter 1 without duplicating Chapter 1's lifecycle argument.

Two constraints attach. Per the accepted `G-A2d` record, Appendix B is
retained, so the preface's pathway language must match what the
clean-installation test actually verifies and promise no more. Per pending
handoff `H-P0-STATE-001`, the preface must promise a checkable calculation
trace rather than visible code, consistent with D05 and H10.

### `G-A2b-I` — Part I, Chapters 1-3

Ratify only the claim, lifecycle, measurement, and literacy definitions that
later parts genuinely depend on. Hold the D05 boundary that Part I shows no
visible code. The opening ramp stays gentle; formal definitional load is not
increased for its own sake.

### `G-A2b-II` — Part II, Chapters 4-6

The load-bearing progression is: the analysis table is constructed rather than
found, then a visual claim must be honest, then association has limits. This
carries the constructed-data thread that Chapters 8 and 16 later harvest.
Visualisation does not lead, and conventional summary-statistics-first order
is explicitly rejected.

### `G-A2b-III` — Part III, Chapters 7-9

Keep probability, then sampling, then estimation. Preserve Chapter 8 as the
book's pedagogical hinge, and organise the part around what generalisation can
and cannot reach. This is where Chapter 3's planted poll and margin-of-error
debts are paid off, per `H-P0-REGISTER-008`.

### `G-A2b-IV` — Part IV, Chapters 10-12

Magnitude and the consequences of error lead. Significance testing is taught
with its history and its abuses rather than as procedure. Chapter 12 remains
one evidence-led argument about the research system rather than a list of
reforms. Testing-mechanics-first order is explicitly rejected.

### `G-A2b-V` — Part V, Chapters 13-17

Preserve Chapter 16 as the synthesis payoff. **Chapter 13 is a prerequisite
for Chapter 17**: its conditional denominators and confusion tables must
precede Chapter 17's fairness and classification material.

This settles the contradiction recorded in `H-P0-REGISTER-004`, where the
review's sample critical-literacy route skipped Chapter 13 while the same
review required Chapter 13 before Chapter 17. The consequence is binding on
`P2-SPINE-V` and `P5-ROUTES`: the advertised short route must be amended, and
no route may send a reader into Chapter 17 without Chapter 13.

### `G-A2b-FINALE` — Chapter 18

Chapter 18 is whole-book cumulative and harvests every thread through one
empirical transfer task the student audits end to end, including auditing an
assistant's report on the same analysis. This is where the seventh
communication thread lands.

**Amended from the recommended default.** Chapter 18 *may* introduce a
technique the worked case genuinely requires, under three exact limits:

1. never a method on the preface's out-of-scope list — time series, factor
   analysis and psychometrics, multilevel models, the mathematics of machine
   learning, or full Bayesian inference;
2. fully explained where it appears, self-contained;
3. no forward dependency — it may not require anything that no earlier spine
   was asked to supply.

`P2-SPINE-FINALE` and `P6-CONTINUITY` must both enforce these limits. An
unbounded new method in the capstone would reopen the scope promise the
preface makes.

## `G-A2c` — terminology

One canonical Croatian form per concept, used everywhere. English retained in
the glossary. Accepted variants and any deliberate departure from Croatian
convention recorded explicitly rather than left implicit.

The author accepted the disposition that **includes an independent reviewer**:
a Croatia-based social-science methods reviewer who did not author the term
registry, per `OA-P6-TERMS-REVIEWER-RECRUIT`. That is a real recruitment task
with lead time no other item on this list has, and it is not satisfied by any
intent recorded here.

This gate remains correctly sequenced after spine ratification: canonical terms
can only be fixed once the spines have determined which concepts are
load-bearing.

## `G-A3-DZS` — Croatian official statistics, selection

Complete-year national monthly arrivals and nights, one county cross-section,
and the bounded long extract, preserving totals and suppression semantics. The
source complications are curriculum rather than noise: an arrival is a
registration and not a distinct person, geography mixes levels, and
confidential or unpublished cells differ from zero.

Selection only. `OA-G-A3-DZS-RIGHTS` remains open and is owned by DZS.
Technical availability is not redistribution authority, per `H-P1B-DATA-LIC-003`.

## `G-A3-DIP` — election data, selection

A municipality-or-unit extract from the 2024 parliamentary election, frozen as
a historical dataset rather than silently replaced after the next election.
Turnout numerators and denominators are retained, and every teaching total
reconciles to the official source. Serves Chapters 1, 3, and 13 with a domestic
case for turnout, vote shares, categorical tables, and the ecological fallacy.

Selection only. `OA-G-A3-DIP-RIGHTS` remains open and is owned by DIP.

## Sequencing note

Nine of these ten intents match the recommended default already recorded in the
register. The single amendment is `G-A2b-FINALE`, which permits a bounded new
method in the capstone where the default forbids any.

The two rights asks are the only items on the critical path whose timing the
author does not control, and they may be sent ahead of their gates once the
author authorises external contact. No external message has been sent on this
project to date.
