# Author amendment — WD-C14 and C14 preparation

**Decision ID:** `A-THREAD-WD-C14-C14-PREP-2026-08-13`

**Decision date:** 13 August 2026

**Recorded:** 17 August 2026

**Author and editor:** Luka Sikic

## Exact amendment

For this thread only, the ordinary stop-after-one-packet rule is replaced by
one strict two-packet chain. `WD-C14` must first be claimed, completed,
evidenced, handoff-disposed, workflow-checked, closed and locally committed.
Only then may `C14` be claimed for preparation of its complete acceptance
package.

`C14` may not close while the author is away. The packet must stop after it
has assembled evidence tied to the final Chapter 14 source commit, all six
critic reports, the synthesis, the applied D02 disposition, every unresolved
or deliberately displayed minor finding, and the ledger disposition that
would be applied on acceptance. All six critics must address that exact final
material state. The only reply that can satisfy the author-acceptance exit test
is

```text
C14 accepted for <commit> on <date>
```

The standing delegation of 5 August 2026 cannot substitute for that reply.
The gate must remain open while the reply is absent. `WD-C15` and every later
packet remain prohibited.

## Packet boundaries

Each packet keeps its own claim, one write lock, evidence bundle, handoff
dispositions, workflow checks and commit. No evidence, unfinished disposition
or second write lock may cross the boundary. The amendment waives no packet,
item, handoff, panel, deterministic check or prerequisite.

`WD-C14` implements the ratified D02 correction specification exactly, follows
the ratified `G-A2b-V` spine, consumes `H-P3-ESS-001` before claim, acknowledges
`H-WB-C06-001` before its first substantive edit and consumes it before
closeout. ESS remains optional, reader-owned, portal-mediated and unpromoted;
the mandatory offline task uses the licensed local alternative. A full six-
critic panel must read one final material state, and every fatal or major
finding must be resolved before closeout.

Chapter 6 remains deliberately `draft` under `H-WB-PART-001`; Chapters 7–12
remain accepted under `C07-C12-REACCEPT`. The thread changes neither state.

## Stop conditions and authority boundary

Stop if D02 cannot be implemented as ratified, if real evidence cannot satisfy
an exit test, if a fatal or unresolved major finding remains, or if completion
would require inventing a number, source, study or citation. The data, reading-
time, reader-validation, terminology-review and rights boundaries already in
the register remain binding.

This amendment authorises only the named strict chain through preparation of
`C14`. It does not authorise `C14` acceptance, `WD-C15`, a second write lock,
an author-reading claim, push, merge, tag, archive, deployment, publication or
any external action.
