## Exact amendment

For this thread only, the ordinary stop-after-one-packet rule is replaced by
the strict three-packet chain `WD-C15 -> C15 -> G-A4-16`. This is a new,
distinct decision. The seven earlier thread-amendment chains have all ended on
their own terms and supply no continuing authority.

Each packet must be independently claimed, evidenced, handoff-disposed,
workflow-checked, closed and locally committed before the next packet is
claimed. At most one write packet may be active. No lock, evidence or
unfinished disposition crosses a packet boundary, and no packet or item
prerequisite is waived.

## WD-C15 boundary

WD-C15 revises Chapter 15 as one complete vertical slice against the ratified
`G-A2b-V` spine. Shared vocabulary conforms to accepted Chapter 14 and the
ratified `G-A2c` terminology register; the packet coins no new multiple-
comparison term. Magnitude and error consequences lead. The omnibus result is
not the payoff: it opens the question answered by effect sizes and intervals.
The standing R11 deferral permits no multiple-imputation production.

`H-P3-ESS-001` is consumed before claim. ESS Round 11 edition 3.0 remains an
optional, reader-owned, portal-mediated and unpromoted replication route with

across editions, redistribution authority, rights-holder permission or an
official-turnout validation. Mandatory offline work uses the promoted CC BY
4.0 `data/populacija-medija.csv` or
`data/populacija-medija-agregat.csv` instead.

All six independent critics must read one final material source state. Their
synthesis recommends a disposition but records no acceptance. Every fatal and
major finding must be resolved before WD-C15 closes.

C15 requires the complete final WD-C15 source commit, all six critic reports,
their synthesis, every unresolved or deliberately displayed minor finding and


The thread stops while that reply is absent. On receipt, only the bounded C15
acceptance disposition and advancement of `15-vise-grupa` to

## G-A4-16 decision stop

G-A4-16 prepares a complete decision brief following the structural model of
G-A4-03 and G-A4-12. It gives the exact public claim and sources, argument
outline, definition and widget boundary, data and rights boundary, unavailable
claims, alternatives, authority boundary and blocked dependencies.

The brief presents separate recommended defaults and alternatives for one
real, verified published regression table and paragraph in a bounded no-refit
reading task; the exact reproduction or quotation conditions tied to the
proposed representation; and the open binary-outcome bridge decision. The
bridge is either ruled on at G-A4-16 or explicitly deferred to WD-C16 with a
reason. The packet retrieves and promotes nothing, writes no Chapter 16 prose,
infers no permission from availability and does not answer either author-owned
outside ask. It stops for the author's exact decisions. External blockage is
an expected honest outcome.

## Standing boundaries and authority

Chapter 6 remains deliberately `draft` under `H-WB-PART-001`; it is neither
advanced nor panelled here. Chapters 7–12 retain their C07-C12-REACCEPT state.
All DigiKat and Eurostat method, denominator, reach and 2024-gap constraints

new-reader validation, a first-edition independent terminology review or
rights-holder permission that has not been obtained.

Stop before the next packet if an exit test lacks real evidence, a fatal or
unresolved major finding remains, or completion would require inventing a
number, source, study or citation. Stop at the C15 reply and G-A4-16 decision
gates. Honest partial completion is the required outcome.
